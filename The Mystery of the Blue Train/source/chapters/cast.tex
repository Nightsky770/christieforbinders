\addchap{Cast of Characters}

\begin{a4}
	\enlargethispage{\baselineskip}
\end{a4}

\begin{description}
\item[Monsieur le Marquis,] an elegant gentleman who merits his mask but not his white hairs

\item[Demetrius Papopolous,] a venerable dealer in unique antiques

\item[Zia Papopolous,] whose resemblance to her father, Demetrius, is more than physical

\item[Rufus Van Aldin,] a hard man, with plenty of hard cash

\item[Major Richard Knighton,] Van Aldin's secretary—quick, intelligent, and resourceful

\item[Ruth Kettering,] another daughter who resembles her father, Rufus Van Aldin

\item[Derek Kettering,] Ruth's husband, the future Lord Leconbury

\item[Mirelle,] an exotic dancer who was not made to be poor

\item[Katherine Grey,] a grey-eyed saint with a sense of humour—and a brand-new fortune

\item[Viscountess Rosalie Tamplin,] who has an eye (blue and charming) for a quick penny

\item[The Honourable Lenox Tamplin,] who does not resemble her mother

\item[M. Hercule Poirot,] the semiretired Belgian detective who is semimodest about admitting that he is always right

\item[Ada Mason,] lady's maid to Ruth Kettering, and English to the tip of her red nose

\item[Comte Armand de la Roche,] a plausible scoundrel with a fatal fascination for women
\end{description}
