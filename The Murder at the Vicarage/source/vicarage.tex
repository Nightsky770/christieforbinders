\workname{The Murder at the Vicarage} was originally published in 1930 by Collins Crime Club in London (UK) and by Dodd, Mead \& Co. in New York (US).  It was the tenth novel by British author Agatha Christie (1890--1976), and the first to feature detective Miss Marple.

Illustrations are from the 1930 US publication. No artist credit was given in the original. Grateful acknowledgment is made to the Internet Archive, which in 2025 digitised a copy of this edition belonging to the Boston Public Library.
